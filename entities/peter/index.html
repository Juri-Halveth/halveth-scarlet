Family as a relationship with room to grow.

Story and thesis

Peter’s perspective connects family and accountability in the existing essay. HALVETH’s alternative to Ego starts with a relationship that has room to grow, rather than using Peter’s abilities as a means of expansion.

This reading treats family as a lived relationship: support, trust and criticism remain possible. The Guardians section develops that idea as a proposed continuation.
